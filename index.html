How we work

A Florida landlord, and due process matters

HAHA Happy Townhome LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, document number L24000046867, filed in 2024. We let only property we own — managing property for other owners for compensation requires a Florida broker licence, and we do not do it.

We are an equal housing opportunity provider: written criteria, applied the same way to everyone, and reasonable accommodations considered as fair housing law requires. Deposits are held and accounted for under section 83.49, including written notice of any claim within 30 days of a tenancy ending. And possession is recovered only through the court — we do not change locks, remove belongings or interrupt utilities, because that is unlawful whatever the arrears.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

What do I need to qualify?

The criteria are published before you apply — income, rental history, credit and background thresholds — so you can decide whether to spend the application fee. The same test applies to every applicant.

How is my deposit held?

As section 83.49 requires, and you get written notice of how and where. Any claim against it must be made to you in writing within 30 days of the tenancy ending, or the deposit is returned.

Why is there a radon disclosure in the lease?

Florida requires it in any lease over 45 days. It is a state-mandated notice, not something specific to this property, and a lease without it is missing a required disclosure.

Can you evict me without going to court?

No, and nobody can. Changing locks, removing belongings or cutting utilities to force a tenant out is unlawful in Florida regardless of what is owed. Possession comes through the court process only.

Do the association rules apply to me as a tenant?

Yes, and that catches people out. We give you the rules before you sign so you can see what applies to parking, pets and patios rather than meeting them as a fine.

My air conditioning has failed in August. How fast will you act?

Treated as urgent, not routine — the lease states response times by urgency. Florida heat makes that a habitability issue rather than an inconvenience.
