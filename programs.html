Program 02 · Held as the law requires

Leases & deposits

Florida sets out precisely how a deposit must be held, what notice you get about it, and that any claim against it must be made in writing within 30 days of the tenancy ending. We follow that, and the radon disclosure the state requires is in every lease rather than left out.

  • Tenants

Program 03 · One contact, real response

Maintenance

A failed air conditioner in a Florida August is not a routine repair, and the lease says so. We give one contact rather than a portal nobody reads, state response times by urgency, and give notice before entering as the law requires.

Program 04 · Given before you sign

Association rules

Townhomes come with an association, and its rules on parking, pets, patios and amenity access bind tenants too. Handing those over before signing prevents the common surprise of a fine for something nobody mentioned, and tells you honestly whether the rules suit how you live.

  • Tenants
